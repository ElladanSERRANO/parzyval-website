[Intro]

[Verse 1]

Neon sweat and heavy sound,
We owned the night, we held the ground,
Laughing loud, no looking back,
Didn’t hear the system crack.
Lights flickered—one, then two,
Something in the voltage grew,
Music choked, the silence split,
Then the darkness swallowed it.

[Pre-Chorus]

One scream cut through the bass,
Not a show—this was a break,
The kind of cry that scars the air,
The kind you never shake.

[Chorus]

Red lights! Flood the floor!
Guns at the door! We’re at war!
The rhythm died in a hail of fire,
Hope hit the ground with the choir!
We walked in loud, we crawled out raw,
Blood on the tiles, teeth in the jaw,
The night we loved turned into flame—
And nothing, nothing’s the same!

[Verse 2]

Bodies where the dancers stood,
Chaos tasting like burnt wood,
Move or fall, don’t hesitate,
Fight the dark or seal your fate.
One shot close, too close to breathe,
Smile through pain you don’t concede,
“Go!” through clenched and bleeding teeth,
But mercy packed its bags and left.

[Pre-Chorus]

Four cracks—red light!!
A body drops out of sight,
A scream breaks skin and bone,
And suddenly you’re alone!

[Chorus]

Red lights! Sirens scream!
Shattered glass and broken dreams!
The music drowned in powder smoke,
Everything holy snapped and broke!
We held the wound with shaking hands,
Begged the night to understand,
But fate kicked down the door that day—
And ripped the world away!

[Guitar Solo 1]

[Bridge]

Blood on my hands, not mine—
Breath counting down in time,
Sirens wail, hearts collide,
Something in me fucking died.

Someone stood in front of death,
Three loud cracks, one final breath,
Heroes fall the way they must—
Steel and fire, bone and dust.

[Final Chorus]

Red lights! Burn it in!
Let the nightmare crawl under skin!
We walked in young, we walked in proud,
We left in silence, screaming loud!
This city hums like nothing changed,
But we were split, rearranged,
The night was torn, the line was drawn—
And we’re not who we were at dawn!

[Guitar Solo 2]

[Outro]

The lights still flash behind my eyes…
The shots still echo in my mind…
You don’t walk out of nights like this—
You just survive them.
